Heey!

My name is Filip Kockendal, and I'm a product designer currently improving clinical workflows for practitioners across the Nordics at Nordhealth. I've hopped around a bit across different domains, from finance to clinical workflows and AI initiatives. It's hard to say what I enjoy the most, but it's safe to say that I like trying new things.

In my spare time, I enjoy doing a bit of creative coding and exploring ways to make fun interactive art. I'm still learning, but it's really fun! Something I want to get into more is the intersection between interactive art and sound design.

Another hobby I've had for some time is playing guitar. I still haven't tried playing an electric guitar, but hopefully at some point! I'd also love to try playing the contrabass; I've tried it briefly, but definitely want to learn more. PS: it's a beautiful thing to have a hobby even though you're poor at it, so expect nothing fancy :-D
