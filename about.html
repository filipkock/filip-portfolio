I am Filip Kockendal, a product designer and creative coder based in Copenhagen. By day I design healthcare software: scheduling, journals, and clinical workflows for practitioners across the Nordics.

The rest of the time I draw with code. Parametric grids, dithered fields, and recursive subdivisions, in black and white, made to survive a pen plotter. This site is one of those systems: every canvas on it is drawn live from a seed.
